# ADR 0003: Kuvend national advisory product

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-02

## Decision

The public product is Kuvend at `kuvend.org`. It is independent and non-governmental. International phone control is accepted for participation, so results are national advisory signals rather than citizenship-, residence-, or election-verified outcomes.

Every eligible proposal receives a 14-day vote after moderation. Votes are final `support` or `oppose` choices. Turnout is visible before voting, the live split is visible to a participant after voting, and closed results are public.

## Consequences

The interface stays simple and avoids a separate support-threshold phase. The public copy must state the representativeness limitation wherever results appear.
