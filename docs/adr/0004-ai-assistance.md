# ADR 0004: AI edits but does not author or decide

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-02

## Decision

AI assistance is optional, side-by-side, and participant-approved. It may correct, simplify, translate, transcribe, and suggest similar proposals. It cannot publish, invent facts, rank, merge, or moderate.

## Consequences

Drafts are sensitive ephemeral content. Provider-backed assistance is restricted to synthetic or explicitly low-risk use until zero-retention or self-hosted processing is approved.
