# AI writing assistance policy

Kuvend uses AI only as an optional editor. The participant remains the author and decision-maker.

## Permitted

- spelling and grammar correction;
- plain-language simplification;
- title suggestions;
- speech transcription after the voice privacy gate is met;
- clearly marked English translation from authoritative Albanian text;
- suggestions of similar public proposals.

## Prohibited

- inventing facts, evidence, costs, people, or institutions;
- changing the political direction or intensity of a proposal;
- publishing without explicit approval;
- ranking, accepting, rejecting, merging, or moderating proposals;
- sending phone numbers, credentials, IP addresses, or stable participant identifiers to a model provider;
- retaining discarded drafts or audio.

The interface shows original and suggested text together with `Keep my text` and `Use this version`. Similarity is advisory and never silently merges content.

Sensitive use requires self-hosted processing or an approved zero-data-retention arrangement and review by native Albanian speakers across dialects, regions, ages, and education levels.
