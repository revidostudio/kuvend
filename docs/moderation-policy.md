# Moderation and appeals

Moderation protects participation without deciding whether an idea is politically desirable.

## Acceptance checks

A proposal must concern an actionable public issue, contain enough detail to understand the requested change, avoid targeted personal data, and not be a confirmed duplicate.

## Removal grounds

Threats, unlawful content, targeted personal data, impersonation, coordinated spam, deceptive evidence, and content with no actionable public-policy connection may be rejected. Political disagreement is not a removal ground.

Every rejection states a rule and reason. Duplicate decisions link to the primary proposal. The author may revise or appeal through the private capability secret. Appeals and high-risk removals require two moderators before a sensitive launch.

Policy changes are versioned publicly. Emergency rules expire unless adopted through normal governance.
