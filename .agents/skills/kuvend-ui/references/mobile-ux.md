# Mobile UX rules

- Design and test 320×568 first; enhance at 640, 768, and desktop widths.
- Use dedicated list/detail screens on mobile. Do not squeeze desktop split panes.
- Controls are full-width when it reduces choice or typing effort. Touch targets are at least 44×44.
- Use bottom drawers below 640px and dialogs above. Sticky actions respect `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
- Wizards reveal one decision at a time and end with a literal confirmation screen.
- Keep headings within 30–34px on phone detail screens. Use short lines and visible section dividers.
- Verify browser back behavior, keyboard visibility, 200% zoom, reduced motion, long Albanian words, slow networks, and no horizontal overflow.
- Turnout may be visible before voting; the vote split must remain hidden until the final vote is accepted.
